Hood play ("wiggle") test, 2026-10-25 ~17:50, for the next hood revision
=======================================================================

Setup: Odin 2 Portal in the printed hood (as printed, "sigma 35mm to
odin2portal adapter.step"), A7 III + Sigma 35 mm at f/2.8, defocused as for
measuring. murdii nudged the Odin by its USB cable between shots and let
go; one full-screen grey 128 shot at backlight 177 per position (the shirt
covering the USB opening was off; irrelevant for edge positions).

Method (wiggle.py): the four screen edges located at 13 points each where
the light crosses half way (sub-pixel; works on defocused edges, the blur is
symmetric), a line fitted through each; centre, width and tilt from the
lines. Scale: the panel's active width, 154.9 mm (7", 16:9) = 4136 px in
the frame, so 26.7 px/mm; resolution well under 0.1 mm.
x = along the screen's long side (left/right in the frame),
y = along its short side (up/down in the frame), tilt = rotation in the
screen plane (positive = as the frame shows it, clockwise-ish).

                   i.e. a fraction of a millimetre towards/away from the lens

Reading it:
  - The Odin slides more than it twists in plain pushes (1-3 kept the tilt
    within 0.03 deg); twisting it on purpose (4-6) turned it by up to
    +-1.2 deg around the start.
  - 2.4 deg of rotation over the device's length (~240 mm body) is roughly
    +-2.5 mm at its ends: the same clearance as the 2.5 mm in y, so it is one
    gap, used as slide or as twist.
  - The start position was not centred in the play (x mostly to one side,
    y mostly to the other): it rests wherever it was last pushed.

For the locating pins / ribs of the next revision:
  - Total clearance today ~2.0 mm along the long side, ~2.5 mm along the
    short side.
  - To bring it to ~0.3 mm total (a slide fit for a print, ~0.15 mm per
    side), the locating features should take up about 0.85 mm per side along
    the long side and about 1.1 mm per side along the short side; check
    against the real print tolerance with a test piece first.
  - Locate the short side at both ends (or pins near the device's corners):
    that stops the twist, which is the larger effect at the ends.
  - The camera itself sits ~1.5 deg rotated against the screen and slightly
    off perpendicular (top/bottom edges tilt ~1.8 deg, left/right ~0.75 deg
    in the morning shots, a keystone): worth a look at how the lens mount
    seats too.
